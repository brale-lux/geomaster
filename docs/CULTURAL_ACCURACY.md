# Cultural accuracy of character depictions

Requirements set by the owner on 2026-11-17. They are stored per person in
`historical_people.depiction_brief` and apply to every role and to any future (3D) redraw.
`character_art_assets.cultural_review_status` stays `pending` until a qualified reviewer signs off;
nothing in the pipeline marks it approved automatically.
